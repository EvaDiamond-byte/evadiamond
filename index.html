What is EvaDiamond BioIntel?

BioIntel helps users research public biographies without turning missing information into assumptions. It combines structured public records with AI-assisted analysis and is designed to surface sources alongside the result.

From a name to an organized public profile.

Ask about a person, select a research area, and BioIntel can return a concise research response with available sources. Future search connectors can expand this to reputable news, official, academic and institutional sources.

Designed for:
✓ Biographical research
✓ Career and education research
✓ Public achievements and awards
✓ Chronological life timelines
✓ Publicly documented spouse/relationship information
✓ Source discovery and evidence review

Not designed for:
Private addresses, passwords, financial records, private medical data or other sensitive personal information.
